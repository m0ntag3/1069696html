How a match happens

  1. 01

    Read a listing

    Age, temperament, the desk that has them, and what sort of house will actually work.

  2. 02

    Send a note

    A short application: where you live, who else is there, and why this animal.

  3. 03

    Meet at the desk

    The centre reads what you wrote and arranges a visit. The care fee is paid only if you both say yes.
